Shri Tantra Ganapathi Swami Vari Alayam is one of the most sacred shrines in Sanatan Dharma. Lord Ganapathi, the remover of obstacles, resides here in Tantra form, bestowing knowledge, wealth, and the path to liberation upon devotees. Vakratunda Mahakaya Suryakoti Samaprabha — Nirvighnam Kuru Me Deva Sarva Karyeshu Sarvada 🙏
